Four easy spots for an evening with friends in Kampala

All four are relaxed rather than loud, all have parking, and three sit on Tank Hill in Muyenga. Prices where a venue publishes them are shown in the currency on its own menu. Treat them as a guide, not a quote.

The four

4.5 stars, 346 reviews - best all-rounder

Mojjo

Rooftop on the Tank Hill Parade Building in Muyenga. Open to the sky, cooler and quieter than the road below, and one of the few places built for a long unhurried evening. Friday acoustic sets from 6pm with free entry. They roast their own coffee on site and cook to order.

Location: Tank Hill Parade Building, Tank Hill Road, Muyenga. Price guide: food and drinks priced in shillings on the house menu; water comes free to the table. Best for: a long sit-down evening with music you can still talk over.

Mojjo rooftop, Muyenga - www.mojjokla.com
4.3 stars, 769 reviews - the liveliest of the four

Tales Lounge and Gardens

Bukoto Kisasi Road, just past Kirabo Complex. Gardens, gazebos, a fire pit for the evening and generous parking. Karaoke on Tuesdays, a live band on Thursdays, DJs at the weekend. The kitchen does proper meals and meat platters. Service slows down when it fills.

Location: Plot 1984 Bukoto Kisasi Road, next to Kirabo Complex. Price guide: meals and platters priced in shillings on the house menu. Best for: a group that wants food, music and room to spread out.

Tales Lounge, Bukoto Kisasi - kagwelawa.com
4.3 stars, 338 reviews - quietest, best for talking

Horus Lounge

Rooftop at 24 Tank Hill Road, Muyenga. Low lighting, a soft playlist and a city view that comes good as the sun drops. Built around cocktails and margaritas, and the food menu is deliberately small, so eat before you go if you are hungry. Parking is tight on busy nights.

Location: 24 Tank Hill Road, Muyenga. Price guide: cocktail-led, priced in shillings on the house menu. Open until 3am daily. Best for: a slow evening of conversation rather than a big meal.

Horus Lounge, Tank Hill Road - kagwelawa.com
4.3 stars, 2194 reviews - best food and drink menu

Mediterraneo at Villa Kololo

Windsor Crescent, Kololo, behind a lit courtyard of pink flowers. Wood-fired pizza, pasta and grilled dishes, a serious wine cellar with sommeliers who will walk you through it, and a well-priced cocktail list. Dearer than the Muyenga rooftops, and worth it if the evening is about eating properly.

Location: 42 Windsor Crescent, Kololo. Verified prices from their own menu: local beer 10, soft drinks 5, cocktails from 25 (Mojito 25, Margarita 28, Old Fashioned 35). Kitchen closes at 10pm. Best for: dinner first, drinks after, home at a reasonable hour.

Mediterraneo at Villa Kololo - www.aldearestaurant.co.ug

If you only pick one

Mojjo for a long relaxed evening with music. Tales if the group wants food and a livelier night. Horus when the point is the conversation. Mediterraneo when dinner is the plan.
