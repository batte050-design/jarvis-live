Type: flagship tablet with wireless VCI. Sale price US$1,569, was US$1,599. The step you buy when you are also doing coding and programming work, not inspection alone.

For inspection work specifically

The business scans cars pre-purchase and diagnoses faults. That needs all-system reading, live data, freeze frame and a few resets, not ECU coding. The MK808S covers it for roughly a quarter of the MS906 Pro; the Launch costs about the same and carries slightly more reset services but a weaker build and a smaller update window. Land the MS906 Pro only when customers start paying for coding and key work.
