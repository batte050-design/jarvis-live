What actually changed this year

CAN FD and DoIP stopped being premium features and became the floor; a tool without them cannot talk to a 2024 or later vehicle. Topology mapping, a picture of every module's communication state, is now standard from about $1,200 up. The money moved to the subscription: Topdon publishes $795 a year after the included period, and Autel and Launch gate new model years the same way.

The call for your business

Inspection work needs a full-system scan, live data, bidirectional tests and an exportable report. It does not need ECU programming, an oscilloscope or an ADAS rig. The Ultra S2 is six times the XT90 for capability a pre-purchase inspection never uses, and the COMPASS in the plan costs 2.4M UGX more than the XT90 while adding Tesla, BMW test plans and a slightly larger screen. Buy the XT90 and put the difference into the update subscription and a second battery.

Launch's current flagship is the X-431 PAD9 LINK, with a new SmartLink C V3.0 interface and multi-channel scanning; it publishes no price through an official store, so it is not comparable in this table. Distributor quotes for the outgoing PAD7 LINK ran from about $2,400 to $3,200, which tells you nothing reliable about the PAD9.

Nothing here is a Kampala price. Duty, VAT and freight on a 4 to 12 kg tool are unverified, and none of these makers names an East African dealer, so the landed figure will be higher and a warranty claim will mean shipping the unit out.

Verify the update period in writing before paying: XTOOL publishes none on its US store, and an expired subscription turns a scanner into a brick for anything newer than its last database.
