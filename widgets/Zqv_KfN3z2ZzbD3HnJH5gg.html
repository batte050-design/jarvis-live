iPhone 18 Pro Max vs Galaxy S26 Ultra

I checked the specs and the benchmark tables; the written verdict comes from one independent test lab, so treat the judgements as theirs, not measurements I made.

Head to head

$1,299 / 256GB

iPhone 18 Pro Max

A20 Pro (2nm), 12GB RAM. 6.9in LTPO OLED at 1320x2868 and 460ppi. 249g, IP68 to 6m for 30 minutes. 5,391mAh.

Endurance 19h20 use, 55min full charge at 54W. AnTuTu 11: 3,463,072; Geekbench 6: 12,794. 48MP main with variable aperture f/1.48 to f/4.0, 4x tele at 100mm, 8x optical-quality, 18MP 20mm selfie.

$945ish / 256GB

Galaxy S26 Ultra

Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy, 12GB RAM. 6.9in LTPO AMOLED at 1440x3120 and 500ppi. 214g, IP68 to 1.5m. 5,000mAh.

Endurance 16h22 use, 43min full charge at 60W, plus reverse wireless. AnTuTu 11: 3,892,165; Geekbench 6: 11,566. 200MP main at fixed f/1.4, 5x tele at 111mm, 12MP selfie. S Pen in the box.

Who wins each one

Design: the Galaxy is 35g lighter and reads cleaner. Display: the Galaxy, on resolution, a smaller cutout and a privacy mode. Battery: the iPhone by nearly three hours. Charging: a tie in the first 30 minutes, the Galaxy takes the full charge. Performance: the iPhone, on CPU, GPU and sustained load.

Camera: split. The iPhone holds daylight video and low light above 2x; the Galaxy takes the bright 10x telephoto, the ultrawide in the dark, the selfies and the most detail at pixel level on its 5x. Price: the Galaxy by roughly a third.

If you want the longer day, cleaner sustained speed and the better video, it is the iPhone. If you want the S Pen, a bigger screen for the same shell, best-in-class selfies and a lower price, it is the Galaxy.
