Three automotive scanners, three different jobs

For the 2010 Land Cruiser Prado, the cheap Bluetooth dongle is enough to read engine codes and see live data; neither it nor the mid-range tablet will program a Toyota key, and the Launch is the only one that codes modules at all.

The three

about $90

BlueDriver Pro

A Bluetooth dongle with no screen of its own; it runs through your phone app. Reads and clears engine codes plus ABS, SRS and airbag on the big makes, with live data, Mode 6 and a repair-report database.

No subscription and no add-ons. No bidirectional tests, so it cannot drive a component on command. Cheapest by a mile and the easiest to carry.

about $380 for the MK808S

Autel MaxiCOM MK808

A standalone Android tablet with a 7-inch screen, all-system reads, active tests and the common reset services: service intervals, parking brake, steering angle, DPF, injectors, battery registration, TPMS relearn.

A one-off purchase; you buy update subscriptions as they expire. Autel warns the original MK808 is older and shallower than the MK808S now sold, so the S matters.

about $900 to $930

Launch X431 V+

A workshop tablet for those who need more than a reader: 10.1-inch screen, VIN auto-read, 41+ service functions, bidirectional control, ECU coding, topology mapping, and 98% car coverage.

Two years of free updates, then a paid renewal. Imports 12V light trucks, and 24V trucks with an extra module you buy separately. FCA and VAG security-gateway access needs an in-app licence.

What each one actually reaches

Programming a key or matching a module after replacement is the line that separates them. The X431 V+ codes ECUs; the MK808 resets service functions but is not a key or module programmer; the BlueDriver only reads and clears.

On a single Prado: the BlueDriver hands you the fault code, the live data and a suggested fix for under a hundred dollars, and that is the whole job. Step up to the MK808 only if you want the resets that need a tablet, and to the Launch only if you are coding keys and modules for other vehicles.
