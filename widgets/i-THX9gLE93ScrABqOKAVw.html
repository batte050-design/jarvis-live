If it clears at 25 percent (finished electronics, HS 85)

Duty 25 percent, everything else the same. US$2,338 to US$2,517. Which band applies turns entirely on how the clearing agent classifies it, and that one decision is worth about UGX 1.1M.

Duty bands from basketadvisory.com, updated July 2026, and rateships.com, read 9 October 2026. Uganda has no duty-free threshold: duty applies from the first dollar.

add 6 percent plus fees

Plus withholding and clearing

A 6 percent withholding tax applies to certain imports and would add roughly UGX 374,000 to UGX 403,000 if charged. Clearing, port and transport typically add tens of percent more on top of the goods value.

Withholding applies by goods and by importer tax status, so treat it as possible rather than certain.

The honest bottom line

Budget UGX 9M to 11M to have it in your hands, on a tool worth US$1,569. Ask the clearing agent for a written landed-cost estimate with the correct HS code before anything ships, because the classification is the single biggest swing and reclassifying after arrival is how importers lose money. On this line of business that spend only pays back if customers start paying for coding and key work; for inspection scanning alone the MK808S does the job at a quarter of this.
