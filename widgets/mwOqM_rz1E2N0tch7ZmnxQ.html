Where the 45-hour number comes from

The iPhone win is in power use, not in capacity. Similar battery volume, 21.75Wh vs 19.46Wh, but Apple's 45-hour video figure burns 0.48Wh per hour against 1.19Wh implied by GSMArena's 16h23 active-use score. Both are manufacturer or published ratings, not a single head-to-head test.

What matters on a phone is mixed use, which drains both faster. Neither figure was measured side by side here.

Extras the S26 Ultra has

A stylus, desktop mode over USB-C, two eSIMs plus two physical SIMs, an ultrasonic fingerprint reader and a privacy screen mode. The Pro Max has none of those, and its US models keep Qualcomm's modem while the rest of the world gets Apple's C2.

Known faults, both in their first months

iPhone 18 Pro Max owners reported random restarts, touchscreen freezes and speaker crackle; iOS 27.0.1 fixed some of it, and some US AT&T units lost cellular and had to be replaced. The S26 Ultra is nine months past its March launch.

Buy the Pro Max for battery, video and the cleaner long-term software story. Buy the S26 Ultra for zoom, stylus, desktop mode and Android flexibility at the same price.
