Which one for you

For a mobile start, the MK808S at about EUR 368 does the job and pays for itself on the first few inspections. If you plan to take on 2017-up vehicles and want the longest software life, the CRP919E at about EUR 402 is the better middle. The MS906 Pro only earns its price once you are selling written inspection reports at a professional rate.
