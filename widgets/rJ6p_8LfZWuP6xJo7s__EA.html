Payment gateway dashboard - illustrative figures

Every number below is illustrative: made up to show the instrument panel, not measured from any live account. Three providers side by side on throughput, latency, webhook reliability and settlement. Gateway pages to the reader: M-Pesa is the mobile-money rail, Flutterwave and Paystack are the card-and-collection aggregators.

Throughput and success rate

Illustrative

M-Pesa

Slower peak rate and a wider failure tail, mostly STK push timeouts when the handset does not answer. Rail operates in fixed local hours, so volume concentrates hard into the working day.

Peak 41 txn/s - 96.8% success

Illustrative

Flutterwave

Highest peak of the three, failures clustered on issuer declines rather than transport. Card and mobile money arrive through the same endpoint, which flattens the integration work.

Peak 68 txn/s - 98.1% success

Illustrative

Paystack

Best success rate and the steadiest curve. Lower peak because the demo routes card traffic first and falls back to the rail, adding one hop under load.

Peak 54 txn/s - 98.6% success

Latency on the authorisation path

Median and 95th percentile, in milliseconds, accepted requests only. The gap between the two columns is the number that matters to a customer standing at a till: the median is what the average payer feels, the p95 is what the unlucky one does.

Settlement, hours after the transaction: M-Pesa same day, Flutterwave T+1 to T+2, Paystack T+1. All illustrative.
