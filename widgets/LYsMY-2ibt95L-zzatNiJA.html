iPhone 18 Pro Max vs Galaxy S26 Ultra

Two 6.9-inch flagships, opposite philosophies: Apple sells the camera and the silicon, Samsung sells the screen, the zoom range and the stylus.

The two machines

Sept 2026 · €1,599 list

iPhone 18 Pro Max

A20 Pro on 2nm with 12GB RAM, and all three rear cameras are 48MP, including a variable f/1.5-4.0 main lens. Charging is the weak point: half in 15 minutes wired, 25W wireless.

249g · 6.9in 1320x2868 · 5391mAh (5567 eSIM) · IP68 6m

Mar 2026 · €945 / $1,100 list

Galaxy S26 Ultra

Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, 200MP main at f/1.4, plus 3x and 5x telephotos in the same body. 60W wired, S Pen, DeX, seven years of updates.

214g · 6.9in 1440x3120 · 5000mAh · IP68 1.5m

Where each one actually wins

The iPhone wins on endurance and video: 19h20m of active use against 16h23m, and ProRes RAW at up to 120fps, which the Samsung cannot match.

The Ultra wins on sharpness, reach, charging and price: 500ppi against 460, a 5x optical zoom against 4x, 60W wired charging against a half-charge in 15 minutes, and €654 less on the list price.

Camera-and-video man: iPhone. Screen, zoom, charging, stylus and money: S26 Ultra.
