Bolt against Faras: what each pays per day

Measured, not published. Every figure below is from his own receipts of 26 Sep 2026 unless it says otherwise; both apps' shares are still single readings.

Keeps 90% - 10% commission

Faras

His first trip, 16,000 fare, 1,600 taken, 14,400 to him. About 8,800 left after Kampala fuel. Still the better platform on share, held in the ledger, not confirmed in writing.

A day: 57,600 gross at four trips of that size, 86,400 at six, before fuel and any empty running.

Keeps 82.7% - tested, not the published 25%

Bolt

His best trip of day one: XL, 39,000 fare, 6,749 commission, 32,253 to him over 3.6 km. About 25,600 left after the fuel with the empty return. The better day comes from the trip, not the percentage.

A day: 129,000 at four XL trips, 258,000 at eight - but at 3.6 km per trip that is 29 km of driving, not a full day's distance.

The honest shape is: four to six Faras-sized trips net 46,000 to 70,000 for the day after fuel, which matches the 29,500 to 59,500 a day already in the plan. Six Bolt XL trips of the same length as the one he took would net about 150,000 - so the platform matters far less than the size of the trips he is offered.

Two things still unproven: Bolt is taken at 17.3% from one receipt, so if the next Bolt payslip shows a higher share these numbers fall; and Faras has never been checked against a written commission rate. Next time either app pays out, the trip fare and the commission are enough to settle it.
