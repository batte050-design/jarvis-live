Which leaves more in a day

It depends on volume, not just the cut. Bolt carries far more riders in Kampala, so a Bolt day usually has more trips and more gross fare; Faras keeps more of each trip but sends fewer. The honest answer: Faras wins on margin per trip, Bolt wins on trips per day, and which nets more depends on how many jobs you actually close.

Rule of thumb: if a day's gross fares are equal, Faras pays better. Bolt only wins when its extra volume beats the 7-point gap.
