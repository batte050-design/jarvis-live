Four relaxed evening spots in Kampala

Checked tonight, 9 Oct 2026. Every place below is a sit-down, talk-easy room rather than a loud club.

Kisementi, above Acacia Mall

Cielo Lounge Rooftop

Fifth-floor open rooftop on The Cube. Skyline views, cocktails, light plates, DJ later on. Go just before sunset for a seat and the view. Relaxed early, fills up from about 10pm.

Open from 4pm. Cocktails 12,000-25,000. Beer 5,000-8,000. Budget 80,000-200,000 each. RSVP 0742 891 326.

Cielo Lounge on Instagram - www.instagram.com
Bandali Rise, Bugolobi

Jazzville Bugolobi

Kampala's first jazz club. Live jazz and soul sets, low lighting, garden seating, a crowd that came to listen. The quietest of the four and the best one for actual conversation.

Open Mon-Sun, live sets usually midweek and Sunday. Cocktails about 10,000 and beer from 4,000 on old advertised nights, so treat as estimate. Entry usually free.

Jazz Ville on Find.ug - find.ug
Spring Road, Bugolobi

The Local, Bugolobi

Ugandan-inspired bar and kitchen, courtyard seating, cocktails and small plates made for grabbing a table and staying. The most casual of the four, and the easiest on the pocket.

Mon-Thu 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM, Fri-Sat to late, Sun 12:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Happy hour Mon-Thu 4:00 to 7:00 PM. 0776077093.

The Local menu and booking - wethelocal.co
Plot 27, Akii Bua Road, Nakasero

Emin Pasha Hotel garden and bar

Boutique colonial hotel behind landscaped gardens, so it is quiet in a way no rooftop is. The calm drink-and-talk choice, and the only one here you can walk to dinner after.

Grounds open daily, bar through the evening. Cocktails 12,000-25,000, beer 5,000-8,000, budget 80,000-150,000 each. Phone 031 2264712.
