Three diagnostic scanners

Prices are street retail from seller listings read 17 Oct 2026, in USD. Nothing here is a Kampala price, and no Uganda dealer is named because I found none I can stand behind.

The three

$389

Autel MaxiCOM MK808S

The solid floor. Full system read on all modules, 28+ service resets, active tests, Android 11 tablet. After year one, updates cost about $100-125 a year, and a lapsed tool freezes at the coverage it had.

$412-449

Launch X431 CRP919E

Best value of the three. Adds ECU coding and newer protocols (CAN FD, DoIP), 35+ resets, 2 years updates free. After that, roughly $165 a year by Launch's own reseller figure.

pricier

Autel MaxiCOM MK906 Pro

The workshop tier; only worth it if this becomes the business. Same family, deeper coverage, far heavier running cost and hardware. I did not confirm its current street price.

The number that actually matters

Hardware is the cheap half. Every one of these is a subscription with a one-off attached, so price it over five years, not at checkout. The difference between the two affordable ones is roughly two years of updates, not the sticker.

For an inspection business that will read codes and resets across mixed imports, the Launch CRP919E is where the money goes first; the MK808S is right only if he wants the simpler interface and will never code anything.
