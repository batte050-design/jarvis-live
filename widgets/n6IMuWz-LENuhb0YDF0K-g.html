Three diagnostic scanners, by what they cost and what they unlock

Prices are USA pricing read today, 17 Oct 2026. Update periods below one year were not readable on any listing, so treat the absence as unknown, not free.

$549

Autel MaxiCOM MK808BT Pro

Entry professional. All systems on 80+ makes, 28+ service resets, bidirectional tests, Bluetooth VCI.

No ECU coding. 1-year warranty. Reads 2017+ FCA gateway.

$1,499

Launch X431 PRO5

Mid tier and the widest toolbox: 60+ services, ECU coding, topology map, J2534 pass-through with the SmartLink C V2.0.

2 years of updates included. VAG guided functions, Porsche and Audi online.

$1,059

Autel MaxiSys MS906 Pro

Best hardware per dollar: Octa-core, 128GB, 8-inch 1920x1200, wireless V200 VCI.

36+ services, ECU coding, DoIP and CAN FD. One listing showed 4.0/5 from 105 buyers.

What separates the middle tier from the cheap one is coding, not diagnostics. All three read every module and run active tests; only the two dearer machines can write configuration back to a module.

What each one actually costs to own

The sticker is not the bill. The MK808BT Pro's warranty is one year, the MS906 Pro's update term was not stated on the listing I read, and the PRO5 bundles two years. Budget a renewal every year or two after that, and do not buy a scanner whose renewal price you have not been quoted in writing.

For vehicle inspection work the MS906 Pro is the pick at $1,059: it codes ECUs like the PRO5 but you keep $440 and get the faster machine. Take the PRO5 only if you need J2534 module programming or heavy-truck coverage. The MK808BT Pro at $549 is the right second tool, not the only one.
