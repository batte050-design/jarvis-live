Three scanners for a mobile inspection business

Handheld, battery-free, all-systems tools that would carry a mobile inspection. Every price below was read from the seller's own product feed tonight, Friday 9 October 2026, and every photo is the maker's own product shot.

$389 — the value pick

Autel MaxiCOM MK808Z

The cheapest of the three that still does the job properly, and the one I would start with. Autel's own page claims all-systems diagnostics, real bidirectional control, 28+ service resets and AutoVIN, on Android 11 with 4GB of RAM, with one year of free software updates and a one-year warranty. Strong: serious function for entry money, no IP restriction, and a carrying case in the box. Weak: thinner coverage on very new cars, and the software updates are paid after the first year.

$549 — best on newer cars

LAUNCH X431 PROS Elite

The Launch equivalent, aimed squarely at independent workshops. Its page claims ECU coding, active tests, and support for the newer vehicle networks (CAN FD and DoIP), reaching engine, transmission, ABS, SRS and body systems across American, European and Asian vehicles. Strong: better placed than the Autel above on modern cars and on coding work, and it ships in a hard case with the connectors. Weak: the seller's own page is hedged — it tells you to confirm the exact procedure and the update period before ordering, so the fine detail is thinner here than on the Autel pages.

$639 — the one with the adapters

Autel MaxiDAS DS808S-BT Kit

A step up the same ladder as the first, and the one built for the cars you will actually meet. Same Android 11 hardware and bidirectional control, plus ECU coding on some makes including BMW, Porsche and VW, and a Bluetooth VCI. The reason to pay more is the kit: it arrives with a set of vehicle adapter connectors, which is what saves you when an older or grey-import car does not speak the standard plug. Strong: adapters included, one year of free updates. Weak: dearest of the three, and the optional camera and battery tester its page mentions are sold separately.
