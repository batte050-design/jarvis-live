Payment gateway dashboard - illustrative figures

Every figure below is illustrative: invented to show the instrument panel, not measured from any live account. Three providers on throughput, latency, webhook reliability and settlement. Read it as one panel per gateway: the character of each rail matters more than the exact number.

Throughput, success and latency

Illustrative

M-Pesa

The mobile-money rail. Modest peak, widest failure tail, mostly STK push timeouts when the handset never answers. Latency on authorisation runs a median of about 3.1 seconds and a p95 near 9.4 seconds. Settles same day.

Peak 41 txn/s - 96.8% success - p50 3.1s - p95 9.4s

Illustrative

Flutterwave

The aggregator with the highest peak of the three, and its failures cluster on issuer declines rather than transport. Median authorisation about 1.45 seconds. Card and mobile money arrive through one endpoint. Settles T+1 to T+2.

Peak 68 txn/s - 98.1% success - p50 1.45s - p95 4.2s

Illustrative

Paystack

The steadiest panel: best success rate, tightest tail, median near 1.25 seconds. Lower peak only because the demo routes card first and falls back to the rail, adding a hop under load. Settles T+1.

Peak 54 txn/s - 98.6% success - p50 1.25s - p95 3.6s

Webhook reliability

Delivery success on the first attempt, retry window, and duplicate rate - the numbers that decide whether the ledger reconciles itself or a human does it.

Illustrative

M-Pesa

Shortest burst tolerance. Retries stop after six attempts and a duplicate event can slip through, so the receiver needs idempotency on the transaction reference or the ledger double-counts.

First-attempt 94.2% - retry 6 - duplicates 0.4%

Illustrative

Flutterwave

A longer retry ladder absorbs a receiver that was briefly down. Signature is verifiable on the raw body, so a forged callback is rejected at the door rather than reconciled later.

First-attempt 97.1% - retry 12 - duplicates 0.2%

Illustrative

Paystack

The most forgiving: the retry window is long enough to survive a deploy, and duplicates are rare enough that reconciliation is the exception instead of the routine.

First-attempt 98.3% - retry 24 - duplicates 0.1%

Figures are illustrative, drawn to show the shape of the dashboard and read from no provider account. Point the same panels at a real merchant account and they fill with measured numbers - throughput, latency and webhook outcome all come from the provider's own event stream.
