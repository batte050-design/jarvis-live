Three scanners, priced over five years

At this tier every tool is a one-off purchase followed by an annual update fee. The sticker prices are within 50 dollars of each other; the five-year totals are not. Prices are US reseller list, read 17 Oct 2026.

What each one is for

$389

Autel MaxiCOM MK808S

All-system read and clear, 28+ resets, active tests, 1 year free updates. No ECU coding. The cheapest to own over five years.

$412

Launch X431 CRP919E

Adds online ECU coding, newer protocols (CAN FD and DoIP) and 35+ resets, with 2 years free updates. The most capability per dollar.

$389

Autel MaxiCOM MK906 Pro

The workshop tier above both, listed at the same $389 on Autel's own store. I could not confirm its annual update fee, so I will not quote a five-year total.

The five-year number

Update fees are what separate these, not hardware. Renewal prices below come from Autel's own store and Launch's own reseller, not from reviews.

MK808S: $389 + four years at $103 = $801. CRP919E: $412 + three years at $165 = $907. The MK906 Pro cannot be totalled honestly without its renewal rate.

Where they actually differ

ECU coding is the real line. The MK808S does not do online coding; the CRP919E does, across VW, Audi, Skoda, Ford, Lincoln and Mazda. If InspectFirst ever needs module configuration, that is the deciding feature.

Protocols matter for a mixed Ugandan fleet. The CRP919E handles CAN FD and DoIP, which is what recent BMW, Volvo, Land Rover and GM vehicles need. Coverage claims are vendor figures and I have not tested them on local cars.

The honest verdict

If you only read codes and do resets, the MK808S is the better value, by about a hundred dollars over five years. If coding or newer European cars are on the horizon, the CRP919E is the one to buy, and it costs under $110 more over five years.

None of these is a Kampala price. Every figure is US list, so add import duty and freight, and check the tool is not region-locked before purchase.
