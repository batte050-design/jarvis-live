iPhone 18 Pro Max vs Galaxy S26 Ultra

The iPhone is the better camera and the better value after Samsung's price rise. The Ultra is the better tool: stylus, 5x optical reach, desktop mode, and storage that keeps its launch price. All figures are US prices read 17 Oct 2026.

Price is the first surprise

$1,299

iPhone 18 Pro Max

$1,299 for 256GB, per Apple's own newsroom post at launch. Same price since September, storage to 2TB.

$1,399

Galaxy S26 Ultra

$1,299 at launch in March, then Samsung raised it to $1,399 on 1 October 2026 because memory prices jumped. The Ultra now costs $100 more than the iPhone.

Where each one actually wins

Camera: Apple ships one 48MP triple with a variable aperture, giving f/1.48 to f/4.0 and 8x optical-quality reach at 200mm. Samsung ships four lenses, a 200MP main and a genuine 5x periscope. Apple's flexibility is in aperture and video; Samsung's is in megapixels and a second optical length.

Screen: the Ultra's 6.9 inch panel is sharper at 500 ppi against the iPhone's 460, and adds the on-device Privacy Display that dims the screen off-axis. The iPhone goes brighter outdoors, at a claimed 3,000 nits against 2,600.

Endurance and body: Apple rates the Pro Max up to 45 hours of video playback, Samsung's 5,000mAh cell charges faster at 60W against Apple's roughly 50% in 15 minutes on 60W. The Ultra is lighter at 214g against 249g, and the iPhone is the thinner of the two by under a millimetre.

What the sameness hides

Both are 120Hz OLED at the same 6.9 inch nominal size, both IP68, both Wi-Fi 7, both Bluetooth 6. The Ultra adds the S Pen and Samsung DeX, and keeps a physical SIM option depending on region; Apple is eSIM only, which matters for how you get a Kampala line onto it.

The honest verdict

If the camera and long-term resale are the point, take the iPhone, and it is now the cheaper of the two. If you want a stylus, true 5x reach and a desktop mode on a TV, the Ultra is the only one that does it, and you pay a premium that did not exist six months ago.

Neither price is a Kampala price. Both are US list, so budget for import duty, and check the SIM arrangement before you pick the iPhone.
