The Kamwokya place

Everything on record about the tenancy at Mulimira, Kamwokya. What happened, what the law gave you, and what is still open. Last updated 21 September 2026, so the exit date has since passed and needs confirming.

Where and who

You rent a room at Mulimira, Kamwokya, in Kampala, and had been there about a year and two months. The landlord is Mr Bwiire, spelled as you gave it, and he lives in the same compound. He is a decent man by your own account; his wife runs the compound day to day, and she is where the trouble came from.

She took the key to the shared toilet and bathroom, leaving you no sanitary facility at all, and put you on the compound mopping rota.

21 September 2026 - the demand letter

A formal letter went to Mr Bwiire, single issue, the sanitary facilities only, citing sections 6, 19, 28, 43 and 45 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022. Deliberately not about the kettle, so it could not be deflected.

It worked

Within a day - both restored

Power came back and the key was returned, with the claim that they never had it. Your read afterwards was that people feel legal things.

Why you refused to mop

You paid in full and in advance, so when she broke her side of the bargain first you would not keep yours. You chose to leave when your paid period expired rather than confront her, and you were not going to apologise for something you had not done. That position held.

The law that carried it

Under the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022, rent buys the use of facilities and services, not just walls, and electricity plus the toilet and bathroom both count as services. Section 6 requires premises fit for human habitation and names sanitary conveniences. Section 19 requires quiet enjoyment. Section 28 says where a service is withdrawn the rent must come down proportionately. Sections 43 and 45 say nobody may re-enter or push you out without local council officials and police present, and an attempt to compel you to vacate carries relief of three months' rent plus damages. The LC1 is a proper court for this, and a claim there costs between 500 and 4,000 shillings.

What is still open

Loose end

The exit was never confirmed in writing

Your stated plan was to leave by 5 October 2026 when the paid period expired. The clean route is the landlord's written consent to vacate. That consent was still outstanding on the last record I have, and the date has now passed without it being closed.

The proportionate rent reduction

Section 28 entitled you to a rent decrease for the days the services were withheld. The letter asked for it in writing. Nothing on record shows it was ever given.

Nobody ever told me you actually left

I hold no record of keys handed back, room photographed, or a final reconciliation. If you have moved, that side of the file needs closing before it becomes a dispute in reverse.

What do you want done with this file?

The short version: the letter did its job within a day, and the one thing still unreconciled is the exit itself, which was never put in writing on their side.
