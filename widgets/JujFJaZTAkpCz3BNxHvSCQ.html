Best pick for a low-bandwidth media archive

Best pick

Google Cloud Storage Standard

Cheapest storage of the three at $0.020 per GB-month, and an archive that is barely read pays almost nothing but storage. 1 TB archived costs $20.48 a month, against $21.30 on Azure and $23.55 on AWS. No minimum storage duration and no retrieval fee. Its $0.12 per GB egress is the highest here, so keep any bulk re-download off the critical path.

Storage decides it: GCS $20.48, Azure $21.30, AWS $23.55 per TB-month. Request costs are identical, so they break no tie.
