Secondhand phone under $300 for work and driving navigation

Three solid picks, all available used well under $300. Specs verified against GSMArena; used prices are US-market estimates for October 2026 and local Kampala prices will differ.

The three options

Newest, best all-round

Google Pixel 7a (2023)

6.1in OLED 90Hz, measured 1070 nits - the brightest of the three in daylight. Tensor G2, 8GB/128GB, 4385 mAh, 18W wired + 7.5W wireless. Android 16, updates running into 2028.

Pros: best camera (real for inspection work), brightest screen, longest software life, newest so least used-cell wear. Cons: smallest battery, slow 18W charging, runs warm under load. Used est. $160-230.

Cheapest

Samsung Galaxy S21 5G (2021)

6.2in AMOLED 120Hz, 1300 nits peak. Snapdragon 888 or Exynos 2100, 8GB/128GB, 4000 mAh, 25W wired + 15W wireless. Software support already ended.

Pros: big smooth screen, fast and wireless charging, cheapest, easy to service and find parts in Kampala. Cons: no more updates, 2021 cells are worn. Used est. $120-180.

Longest support

Apple iPhone 12 (2020)

6.1in OLED 60Hz, 1200 nits peak. A14, 4GB/64GB+, 2815 mAh, 15W MagSafe. iOS updated through iOS 27.

Pros: very reliable GPS, longest support of the three, huge local repair and resale network. Cons: smallest battery here, 60Hz, used units often 80-88% health. Used est. $190-280.

Value read: the S21 is the cheapest way in and the easiest to fix locally, but you buy a 2021 battery and no future updates. The iPhone 12 has the safest support and GPS story but the worst runtime. The Pixel 7a buys the most usable life and the best screen and camera for the money.

For what you do, the Pixel 7a is the pick.
